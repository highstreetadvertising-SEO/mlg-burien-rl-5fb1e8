Frequently Asked Questions: Personal Injury Lawyer in Burien, WA (2026)

1. What does a personal injury lawyer do?

A personal injury lawyer represents individuals who have been injured due to another party's negligence or wrongdoing. They investigate the accident, gather evidence, negotiate with insurance companies, and if necessary, file a lawsuit and represent you in court to secure fair compensation for your injuries, lost wages, medical expenses, and pain and suffering.

2. How much does it cost to hire a personal injury lawyer in Burien?

Most personal injury lawyers in Burien, including Multani Law Group, work on a contingency fee basis. This means you pay nothing upfront. The attorney only collects a percentage of your settlement or verdict if they win your case. This makes quality legal representation accessible to everyone, regardless of financial situation.

3. How long do I have to file a personal injury claim in Washington State?

In Washington State, the statute of limitations for most personal injury claims is three years from the date of the accident. However, certain cases — such as those involving government entities or minors — may have different deadlines. It's important to consult with a personal injury lawyer as soon as possible after your accident to protect your rights.

4. What types of damages can I recover in a personal injury case?

Personal injury victims may be entitled to recover a wide range of damages, including medical bills (past and future), lost income and reduced earning capacity, property damage, pain and suffering, emotional distress, loss of enjoyment of life, and in some cases, punitive damages if the defendant's conduct was especially egregious.

5. Should I speak with the insurance company before hiring a lawyer?

It is strongly advisable not to provide recorded statements or accept any settlement offers from an insurance company before consulting a personal injury attorney. Insurance adjusters are trained to minimize payouts, and anything you say can be used to reduce or deny your claim. An experienced lawyer will handle all insurance communication on your behalf.

6. What should I do immediately after a car accident in Burien?

After a car accident in Burien, you should: call 911 and seek medical attention immediately, document the scene with photos and videos, collect contact and insurance information from all parties involved, get witness contact information, obtain a copy of the police report, and contact a personal injury lawyer as soon as possible to preserve your legal rights.

7. How long does a personal injury case take to resolve?

The timeline for a personal injury case varies significantly depending on the complexity of the case, the severity of injuries, and whether a settlement can be reached out of court. Simple cases may resolve in a few months, while complex litigation can take one to three years or more. Your attorney will give you a realistic timeline based on the specifics of your case.

8. What if I was partially at fault for the accident?

Washington State follows a "pure comparative fault" rule, which means you can still recover compensation even if you were partially at fault for the accident. However, your recovery will be reduced by your percentage of fault. For example, if you were 20% at fault and your damages total $100,000, you could recover up to $80,000. A skilled attorney will work to minimize your assigned fault percentage.

9. Can I handle a personal injury claim on my own without a lawyer?

While it is technically possible to file a personal injury claim without an attorney, studies consistently show that accident victims who hire a lawyer recover significantly more compensation — even after legal fees — than those who negotiate alone. Insurance companies have experienced legal teams working against you, and having your own attorney levels the playing field considerably.

12. How do I know if I have a strong personal injury case?

Strong personal injury cases generally involve clear evidence of another party's negligence, documented injuries with medical records, measurable financial losses, and a causal connection between the accident and your injuries. The best way to assess the strength of your case is to speak with an experienced personal injury attorney who can review the facts and provide an honest evaluation.
